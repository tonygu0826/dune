Match the oil to the application

A hydraulic system, an enclosed gearbox and a compressor place different demands on their lubricant. State the equipment make and model, oil type required by its manufacturer, operating conditions and existing product where known. Manufacturer product data separates applications, specifications and typical properties; those details matter when reviewing a proposed alternative.

References: ExxonMobil / Mobil · ExxonMobil / Mobil · ExxonMobil / Mobil

Use ISO VG as one part of the specification

ISO VG describes an industrial lubricant's viscosity grade using kinematic viscosity at 40 °C. It is distinct from SAE engine-oil grades and NLGI grease consistency. Two ISO VG 46 oils can have different formulations, application recommendations and approvals. A hydraulic-oil request should therefore say hydraulic oil, ISO VG 46, then add the required equipment specification.

References: ExxonMobil / Mobil · ExxonMobil / Mobil · NLGI

What to include in your enquiry

  • Hydraulic, industrial gear or compressor application.
  • Equipment make, model and manufacturer requirement.
  • Requested brand, exact product reference and ISO VG grade.
  • Existing lubricant and any compatibility or changeover requirements.
  • Operating temperature, load or other relevant service conditions.
  • Quantity, pack size, destination and TDS/PDS or SDS request.

Common questions

Are all ISO VG 46 oils equivalent?

No. ISO VG identifies a viscosity grade, not a complete application or performance specification. Confirm the equipment requirement and the exact product's technical data before considering an alternative.

Is industrial gear oil the same as automotive gear oil?

Do not assume so. Specify the equipment and its required lubricant. Industrial gear oils and automotive driveline oils use different application requirements and may describe viscosity under different classification systems.

Related information

Transmission and gear oils

Transmission-oil enquiries should identify the unit being lubricated, not only the vehicle. DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts requests for ATF, CVT, DCT and automotive gear oils by product reference and manufacturer requirement. The transmission or axle specification is the starting point for describing the correct product.

Lubricating greases

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts enquiries for bearing and multipurpose greases in industrial and commercial applications. A useful grease request combines consistency, equipment and operating conditions. If you are replacing a product, identify the existing grease as well as the proposed reference so compatibility can be considered.

Selection guide

A clear lubricant enquiry describes the equipment requirement and the requested product separately. Start with the machine or vehicle documentation, identify the relevant viscosity or consistency system, then record the performance specification. Brand, quantity, packaging and destination complete the commercial request; they do not establish technical suitability.
