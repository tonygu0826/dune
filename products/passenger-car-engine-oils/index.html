Passenger car engine oils

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL handles enquiries for passenger car engine oils by product reference and vehicle requirement. A useful request identifies the required viscosity and performance specification together; a familiar brand or viscosity grade alone is not enough to establish suitability.

Updated 2 November 2026

Applications

  • Passenger car engines
  • Vehicle servicing and lubricant purchasing

Start with the vehicle requirement

Use the vehicle handbook or current manufacturer service information to identify the engine-oil requirement. Include the vehicle model, engine and model year when the product reference is uncertain. API categories and ACEA sequences describe performance requirements; the correct designation depends on the engine and its specified application.

References: American Petroleum Institute · ACEA

Separate viscosity from performance

An SAE engine-oil grade, such as 5W-30, describes viscosity classification. It is not an API category, an ACEA sequence or a manufacturer approval. Two oils displaying the same viscosity grade can carry different performance claims. Check the exact product's current technical data sheet before treating one as an alternative.

References: American Petroleum Institute · ACEA · SAE International

What to include in your enquiry

  • Preferred brand and exact product reference, if known.
  • Vehicle, engine and model year or the manufacturer's oil requirement.
  • Required SAE viscosity grade and API/ACEA designation.
  • Any required manufacturer approval, copied exactly.
  • Quantity, pack size, destination and requested delivery date.
  • Whether you need a TDS/PDS, SDS or both.

Common questions

Are all 5W-30 engine oils interchangeable?

No. A shared viscosity grade does not establish matching API/ACEA performance or manufacturer requirements. Compare the exact product documentation with the vehicle requirement.

Can I request a particular brand?

You can identify SINOPEC or Great Wall Lubricants and a product reference in your enquiry. Brand names identify requested products, not authorised distribution. Availability and specifications are confirmed by quotation.

Selection guide

A clear lubricant enquiry describes the equipment requirement and the requested product separately. Start with the machine or vehicle documentation, identify the relevant viscosity or consistency system, then record the performance specification. Brand, quantity, packaging and destination complete the commercial request; they do not establish technical suitability.
