Transmission and gear oils

Transmission-oil enquiries should identify the unit being lubricated, not only the vehicle. DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts requests for ATF, CVT, DCT and automotive gear oils by product reference and manufacturer requirement. The transmission or axle specification is the starting point for describing the correct product.

Updated 2 November 2026

Applications

  • Automatic and manual transmissions
  • CVT and dual-clutch transmissions
  • Automotive axles and differentials

Specify the transmission-fluid type

ATF means automatic transmission fluid; CVT and DCT identify different transmission designs and their associated fluid requirements. A product described as multi-vehicle still needs to list the relevant application. Do not assume that an ATF can serve a CVT, a dual-clutch transmission or a manual gearbox without checking the exact manufacturer's requirement.

References: ExxonMobil / Mobil

Keep gearbox and engine-oil grades separate

Where an automotive gear-oil SAE grade is specified, it uses the gear-lubricant classification SAE J306 rather than engine-oil classification SAE J300. Similar-looking grade numbers should not be compared as a common scale. Also record the required performance specification and any formal approval; viscosity alone does not establish suitability for a synchronised gearbox or axle.

References: SAE International · SAE International

What to include in your enquiry

  • Vehicle or equipment, transmission or axle make and model.
  • ATF, CVT, DCT, manual transmission or automotive gear application.
  • Exact manufacturer fluid designation and any required approval.
  • Specified viscosity grade or exact product reference.
  • Preferred brand and existing fluid, if known.
  • Quantity, packaging, destination and requested documentation.

Common questions

Does multi-vehicle ATF mean suitable for every transmission?

No. Check the individual product's stated applications against the exact transmission requirement. A broad product description does not confirm suitability for every ATF, CVT or DCT application.

Are a recommendation and a manufacturer approval the same?

No. A supplier's recommendation is distinct from formal manufacturer approval. Ask for the current technical data sheet and keep its approval wording intact when comparing products.

Technical references

References explain industry terminology. They do not establish the specification, approval or availability of an individual product.

Related information

Passenger car engine oils

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL handles enquiries for passenger car engine oils by product reference and vehicle requirement. A useful request identifies the required viscosity and performance specification together; a familiar brand or viscosity grade alone is not enough to establish suitability.

Heavy-duty diesel engine oils

Heavy-duty diesel engine-oil enquiries need more than a viscosity grade. DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts requests for commercial vehicles and off-highway equipment using the engine requirement, preferred product and purchasing details. Identify the engine and any emissions-system requirements so the enquiry describes the intended application clearly.

Industrial lubricants

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts enquiries for hydraulic oils, industrial gear oils and compressor oils. Start with the equipment and its lubrication requirement, then identify the requested brand or reference. An industrial viscosity grade helps describe the request, but it does not make different application types interchangeable.

Selection guide

A clear lubricant enquiry describes the equipment requirement and the requested product separately. Start with the machine or vehicle documentation, identify the relevant viscosity or consistency system, then record the performance specification. Brand, quantity, packaging and destination complete the commercial request; they do not establish technical suitability.
