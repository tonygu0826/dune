Heavy-duty diesel engine oils

Heavy-duty diesel engine-oil enquiries need more than a viscosity grade. DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts requests for commercial vehicles and off-highway equipment using the engine requirement, preferred product and purchasing details. Updated 2 November 2026

Applications

  • Commercial vehicle engines
  • Off-highway equipment engines

Record the engine specification

Copy the required SAE viscosity grade, API category and manufacturer specification from the engine documentation. Include an ACEA heavy-duty designation where the engine manufacturer requires one. Road vehicles and off-highway equipment may have different requirements even when their oils display the same viscosity grade.

References: American Petroleum Institute · ACEA

Do not assume every diesel category substitutes

API explicitly states that FA-4 oils are not interchangeable or backward compatible with CK-4 and the earlier diesel categories listed in its guidance. This is a useful reminder to follow the engine manufacturer's recommendation rather than select by a newer-looking category name. Requested service intervals should also follow the engine and operating conditions, not a general sales description.

References: American Petroleum Institute

What to include in your enquiry

  • Brand and exact product reference, if known.
  • Engine or equipment make, model and application.
  • Required SAE grade, API category and any ACEA designation.
  • Manufacturer specification and relevant aftertreatment requirements.
  • Operating conditions and requested service interval, if relevant.
  • Quantity, packaging, destination and documents required.

Common questions

Can FA-4 replace CK-4 in any diesel engine?

No. API says FA-4 is not interchangeable or backward compatible with CK-4 and specified earlier categories. Use FA-4 only where the engine manufacturer's recommendation permits it.

Does a product enquiry confirm an extended drain interval?

No. A category description does not establish a drain interval or an individual product approval. The engine manufacturer's requirements, duty and applicable product documentation must be checked.

Related information

Passenger car engine oils

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL handles enquiries for passenger car engine oils by product reference and vehicle requirement. A useful request identifies the required viscosity and performance specification together; a familiar brand or viscosity grade alone is not enough to establish suitability.

Transmission and gear oils

Transmission-oil enquiries should identify the unit being lubricated, not only the vehicle. DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts requests for ATF, CVT, DCT and automotive gear oils by product reference and manufacturer requirement. The transmission or axle specification is the starting point for describing the correct product.

Lubricating greases

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL accepts enquiries for bearing and multipurpose greases in industrial and commercial applications. A useful grease request combines consistency, equipment and operating conditions. If you are replacing a product, identify the existing grease as well as the proposed reference so compatibility can be considered.

Selection guide

A clear lubricant enquiry describes the equipment requirement and the requested product separately. Start with the machine or vehicle documentation, identify the relevant viscosity or consistency system, then record the performance specification. Brand, quantity, packaging and destination complete the commercial request; they do not establish technical suitability.
