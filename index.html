A QUICK GUIDE TO PRODUCT GRADES

SAE

Viscosity grades for engine and automotive gear oils use different SAE systems. Follow the grade and system specified for the application.

ISO VG

ISO viscosity grades classify industrial oils by kinematic viscosity at 40 °C. Confirm the grade required by the equipment manufacturer.

NLGI

NLGI grades describe grease consistency, not oil viscosity. Consider the required grade, thickener type and application together.

A manufacturer approval, a performance claim and a recommendation for use are different statements. Confirm the exact product’s current documentation.
