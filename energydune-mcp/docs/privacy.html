MCP privacy notice

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL INC. · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This notice covers the MCP service at {{SERVICE_ORIGIN}}, including tool calls to {{MCP_ENDPOINT}}. It explains the additional processing that occurs when an AI client calls these tools.

The public website’s direct enquiry form uses Cloudflare and Resend for email delivery and is covered by its separate website privacy policy. An MCP tool call sends the supplied arguments to the hosted service immediately, even if no email is ever sent. MCP tools generate unsent drafts; they do not send enquiries through the website’s email-delivery function.

1. Information processed by this service

Company and category tools process their supplied language, search terms and filters. An enquiry draft can include your name, company, email address, product requirements, requested category or brand, product reference, grade, specification or OEM requirement, quantity/packaging, destination and requested document types. Free text can include other information you choose to supply.

Network information, including an IP address and request metadata, may be processed by Cloudflare to deliver the service and protect its infrastructure. For rate limiting, the Worker reads the trusted edge IP header, computes an IP-derived SHA-256 key and passes that key to Cloudflare’s native rate-limit binding. This is pseudonymous processing, not anonymisation. There is no product user account, sign-in or authentication record in this application.

2. Purpose and application storage

The application uses tool arguments to retrieve category information, validate fields and prepare the response you requested. Draft inputs are processed in memory for the current invocation. The application does not write enquiries to a database, create a persistent customer/draft record or intentionally log request bodies.

This is an application-level description, not a promise that no information is stored anywhere. Cloudflare's hosting, security and operational systems may process or retain logs and metadata under the account configuration and its own policies. Muse or another MCP client can separately retain the conversation, arguments and tool results.

3. Information returned to the client

The draft tool returns a structured summary and a complete email body to the calling client. These can repeat your supplied personal information. A short returned mailto URL also contains encoded draft text; URL encoding is not encryption or anonymisation.

When the encoded link would exceed 1,800 characters, it contains only the fixed recipient and subject; the complete body still returns to the client for manual copying. The response identifies the result as draft only, not sent and not persisted by the application.

4. Email and other service providers

The MCP application sends no email, places no order and takes no payment. If you open a draft or copy it into an email application, that application and its providers handle the information you place there. If you choose to send an enquiry to info@energydune.com, the resulting correspondence is separate from the transient MCP tool invocation and is covered by the website/business correspondence policy.

Muse or another client receives the tool results and applies its own privacy controls, retention and, where applicable, model-training settings. Disconnecting a connector does not establish that prior conversations or tool results have been deleted. Consult the current Muse privacy policy, the privacy controls of your chosen client and Cloudflare's privacy information.

5. Access controls and security

The tools are public and unauthenticated. HTTPS protects transport when the service is accessed over its deployed HTTPS origin; it does not make submitted information anonymous. Native rate limiting targets approximately 60 MCP POSTs per minute per IP at each Cloudflare location and is eventually consistent, rather than a strict global quota.

Send only the information needed for the task. Avoid confidential commercial information or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry or search. No method of transmission or processing can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to call the draft tool, omit optional fields, or contact the company directly by email instead. Review the returned draft before choosing whether to send it. Manage any retained conversations and tool history through the relevant client controls. These tools do not provide a conversation or infrastructure-log deletion function. Ask the relevant provider about records it controls independently; for requests concerning this service or hosting records under our control, contact us.

For questions about this MCP service, contact info@energydune.com. If this notice changes, the updated version will be published on this page with its revised date.
