Privacy Policy

DUNE ENERGY GLOBAL INC. · Last updated: October 23, 2026
Version française

This policy explains what information Dune Energy Global Inc. (“Dune Energy Global”, “we”, “us”) collects through this website and by email, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to energydune.com and to enquiries sent to us from this website.

1. Information you send us

This website does not operate user accounts and does not take orders or payments. When you prepare an enquiry, the website opens a draft in your own email application; nothing is transmitted to us until you send that email. If you contact us, we receive the information you include, such as:

  • your name, company name and email address;
  • the product category, brand and product or reference you are interested in;
  • additional specifications you provide, such as viscosity or consistency grade, specification or OEM approval, quantity and packaging, and destination;
  • documents you request, such as technical or safety data sheets (TDS/PDS, SDS); and
  • the contents of your messages and any further information you choose to provide during quotation or supply discussions.

Please do not include confidential commercial information in an initial enquiry. Email is not a fully secure channel.

2. Information collected automatically

This website does not use analytics services, advertising trackers or third-party fonts, and it does not set tracking cookies. Your language preference (English/French) is stored in your own browser so the site displays in the language you chose; it stays on your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) in server logs for security and operational purposes.

3. How we use information

  • to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you;
  • to confirm product availability and specifications and to prepare quotations;
  • to arrange supply and delivery where an order proceeds;
  • to keep business records and meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations; and
  • to protect the security of our website and business.

We do not sell personal information.

4. When we share information

We share information only as needed for the purposes above:

  • with suppliers, carriers and other service providers involved in preparing a quotation or fulfilling an order for you;
  • with professional advisers (such as accountants or legal counsel) under obligations of confidence;
  • where required by law, regulation or a competent authority; and
  • in connection with a sale or reorganisation of our business, subject to applicable law.

5. International processing

Enquiries may concern products, suppliers or destinations in other countries. Where that is the case, the information needed for the quotation or delivery may be processed in those countries, which may have privacy laws that differ from your own.

6. Retention

We keep enquiry and correspondence records for as long as needed for the business relationship, for follow-up quotations, and to meet legal and accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for a particular purpose, by writing to info@energydune.com. We will respond as required by applicable law. If you no longer wish to receive follow-up communications about an enquiry, tell us and we will stop.

9. Children

This website is intended for business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will be posted on this page with its “last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
